## 1. Rule

Summarized in this project's words from the "Suppressive Fire Rule Change" house rule in _Heavy Armor Lizard's Autofire Compendium_ (go-public spec G2: copy no source or RTG text).

1. Suppressive Fire costs an Action and 10 rounds. With fewer than 10 loaded, it cannot be used.
2. It affects everyone **on foot**, **in front of** the shooter, within 25 m/yd (the source gives this as 13 squares), out of cover, and in the shooter's line of sight.
3. Each rolls WILL + Concentration + 1d10 against the shooter's REF + Autofire + 1d10.
4. A target who fails must **immediately** spend their next Move Action, **before** their Action, moving **away from** the shooter's direction toward cover. If one Move cannot reach cover, they must also Run to reach it, or get as close as they can.
5. Once in cover, they may not move out or lean out until their next turn.

The source states its purpose: stop a suppressed target from moving toward the shooter, attacking before taking cover, or popping out of cover to attack. Which other clauses differ from rules as written is to be confirmed against the core rulebook.

## 2. Scope

Requested by Lukasz (2026-10-11): adopt the house rule and make it compatible with Pneuma's Combat Tools.

Pneuma already automates most of Suppressive Fire. This module adds only what Pneuma leaves to the table, and does not build a second suppression workflow.

## 6. Requirements

- **R1.** House rule off: behavior identical to today, with or without Pneuma.
- **R2.** House rule on, sheet path: Suppressive Fire with fewer rounds loaded than its cost is refused before CPR's dialog, with a localized notification that says the house rule refused it, and spends nothing.
- **R3.** House rule on, Pneuma active: when a target becomes Suppressed, its connected owners get a reminder at the start of that target's next turn stating the obligations in S6-S9. Owners who are offline at that moment get nothing.
- **R4.** Later phase (D8). House rule on, Pneuma active: a move that leaves a Suppressed token closer to the shooter than it was at the start of its turn shows a warning. It is never blocked.
- **R5.** Later phase (D8). House rule on, Pneuma active, movement tracking on a square grid: a Suppressed actor attacking before spending movement this turn shows a warning.
- **R6.** No change to Pneuma's card, rolls, or effects. This module only reads Pneuma's flags.
- **R7.** All strings from `lang/en.json`.
